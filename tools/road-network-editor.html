routes.js

Layers: Roads is what characters walk; Rail is what they follow when a beat says by: rail. Each layer has its own junctions (j… / r…); locations are shared.
New segment: click two nodes to connect them.
Bend a segment: in Select mode, click it to select, then click it again where you want a bend. Drag the diamond handles to shape it.
Junctions: use Add junction for forks away from a town; drag to position. Ids show only for the selected junction unless Junction ids is on.
Delete: select a segment, junction, or bend and press Delete. Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes.
Saving: click Save to routes.js… once and pick the project's routes.js; after that every change saves automatically (refresh the app to see it). In browsers without that feature, use copy and paste over the file.
Drag empty map to pan; scroll to zoom.
